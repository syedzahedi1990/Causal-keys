LABEL critic:replication
### would_move_score
I read all four reports. Part B is necessary clean-up work, but on its own it adds about +0.5 to the panel mean. It cannot produce a strong accept unless Parts A and C also succeed.

STATS REVIEWER (5, soundness 3). This reviewer asked for almost exactly this design: new seeds plus two or more new families with scale-free margins (+1), re-scoring with candidate mass per cell (+0.5), and cluster-aware CIs, risk-separated met rates and pinned revisions (+0.25).
- As designed and met: 5 to 6, soundness 3 to 3.5. Two things will be discounted:
  - JB1, JB2 and JB4 in the new families are labelled R (risky) although their prior is about 0.9 or higher.
  - The "fresh" population is the same 480-configuration space. In an illustrative fresh draw (seed 99, not F's seed), 150 of 150 location configurations already occur in U, and 109 of 150 (B,S,X) triples occur in S0 itself.
- With the required changes met (honest risk classes, new wordings and words, interval-based equivalence, a behavioural line for SENTENCE-AFTER, the SENTENCE-NULL control): 6 to 6.5, soundness 4.
- If it fails:
  - JB3 not met in 2 or more new families: about 5.5. The reviewer rewards a clean preregistered boundary, but the contribution narrows.
  - JB9 not met: as written this happens about 38% of the time by sampling noise alone (see the tautology checks). It reads as "does not replicate", an unforced -0.5. It must be fixed.

SKEPTIC (4). The relevant ask was "re-score Tables 14-16 and 22" (+0.5). JB6, JB8 and X1 (the FP32 check of eager vs sdpa) deliver it, so +0.5 at most (4 to 4.5).
- Part B does not touch either of this reviewer's +1 items (non-templated multi-token data; an out-of-sample intervention battery).
- Wording and lexicon variation plus the SENTENCE-NULL control partly answer "CIs over 150 stories from one template say nothing about variation across templates or lexical items". With Parts A and C this could reach 5.
- If it fails:
  - JB6(c) verdicts change under E: soundness -0.5 to -1. The measurement concern is confirmed.
  - JB3 fails in new families: this supports the skeptic's "duplicate detection used however the task requires" reading. No gain.
  - JB1 or JB4 fail in 2 or more families: falls to about 3.

MECHINTERP (4). Rescoring gives +0.5 soundness at most. Overall stays 4 to 4.5: this reviewer's +1 asks are natural data, characterising the flag, and explaining the negative reads, none of which Part B addresses.
- The SENTENCE-NULL control and the N4 replication of the sentence read are mildly relevant: they show the read needs the re-mention and is not just extra text.
- If it fails: no change unless JB1, JB2 or JB4 fail. In that case: major negative.

GENERALIST (4, confidence 3). Part B resolves "7 of 10 are Qwen", the measurement-validity point, and (through X2) the 1.5B/3B competence question.
- The behavioural crossover figure gives a concrete, generation-level statement. Expected 4 to 4.5. Reaching 5 needs the practical-consequence experiment, which is not Part B.
- Only if a behavioural line for SENTENCE-AFTER is added can the paper say what the sentence read does to answers. The in-hand proxy is β_K(POST) = 0.05 / 0.27 / 0.30 / 0.02 (Qwen-7B, Qwen-14B, Mistral, OLMo), against 0.00 for SENTENCE-BEFORE.
- If JB5 fails: -0.5. The log-probability account would not translate into behaviour.

NET. As designed, about +0.4 on the panel mean, with the stats reviewer as the main gain. With the required changes, about +0.6, with less risk of unforced NOT MET lines.
