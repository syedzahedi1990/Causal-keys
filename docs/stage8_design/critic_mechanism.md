- pilot_critic.py and pilot_critic2.py: Qwen2.5-0.5B, flag fit on 24-30 fresh Random(0) stories (indices 16 and up), evaluated on 24 Random(7) stories, about 5 min each.
- pilot_kv_inout.py: Qwen2.5-1.5B, 16 Random(9) stories, Q_OUT/Q_IN/P1 with K and V clamps and a non-candidate key K_N, about 14 min.

R1 (mech-interp, 4; contribution 2). This reviewer asked for exactly D1 (+1) and for "same readers feeding suppression heads" (+0.5 to +1).
- If D1-D4 and D8-IOI come out as predicted, in the identity-contrast currency, with the leave-one-word-out (LOO) flag and the IOI route test I require below, I expect +1 (4 to 5) and contribution 2 to 3.
- Not more than +1: the flag is Wang et al.'s duplicate signal made rank-1 per layer, Q_IN/Q_OUT is a constructed task, and the "suppression-type downstream reader" stays exploratory (E3/E4). It gets to +1.5 only if the hop-2 reader of the negative IOI read is localised by a preregistered test.
- If D1 or D3 fails at 7B: 0. The 0.5B pilots do not count for this reviewer.

R2 (sceptic, 4). The main objections (Section 4 rests on one remap, templated data) are not part D's.
- Part D answers their "lookup over-reads" point and their IOI-sign question.
- As designed: about +0.25. With the K_N test they asked for, the address-only (K = KV) test, and an honest scope on BEFORE: +0.5.
- Their competence and dissociation point (minor) is fixed by D6's competent cores.
- If D7, D9 and D6 are sold as "risky preregistered sign predictions", this reviewer will see that they are implied by data in hand, which costs credibility (−0.25).
