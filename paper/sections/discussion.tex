\paragraph{What to do differently.}
(1)~Validate patching, steering and KV-cache edits in the format in which the behaviour is used: with the candidates listed after the context, a late patch that changes nearly every free-form answer leaves most listed answers unchanged (\cref{sec:claim3}); localisation methods \citep{mueller2025mib} and an edit's apparent channel (\cref{app:remap}) need the same check.
(2)~With the candidates listed after the context, measure the window per model in that format and edit well inside it (here it has closed by $l_b$, at \NqLbFracMin--\NqLbFracMax{} of the depth); there, a delivered key share well below the natural one predicts a miss. Steering at every position reached named-list answers at every block we tried, but writes at the answer and mostly missed lettered ones (post hoc).
(3)~State the format before reading a layer-wise patching curve, or the answer's attention, at a context token as where the context is read: cutting the answer's attention to the writing token leaves the answer intact with a later list, while without one most answers fail (discovery sample; \cref{app:g-knockout}).

\paragraph{How the predictions fared.}
The risky predictions of the final preregistration held less often than expected (\cref{tab:status}), within chance under the recorded priors (probability \JxRiskyTailP{} of so few or fewer if independent; post hoc), and \JxRiskyMetABCDAlt{} of \JxRiskyVerdABCDAlt{} (expected \JxRiskyExpABCDAlt{}) under the stricter of two conflicting evaluability rules; the extrapolations fell short only for the channel-ratio law (\cref{app:prereg-j}). Every failure of the final round is listed in \cref{app:failures-j}, and those of the earlier entries, among them a transfer law for interventions and a key-share threshold, in \cref{app:prereg-ai}. Most of the stage-9 note's lines were not supported, two only through a single cell near its threshold (\cref{sec:claim3}).

\paragraph{Limitations.}\label{sec:limitations}
(i)~Our passages are counterfactual SQuAD items with added options and the answer named once, scored at the first answer token; naturally occurring later mentions, with the counterfactual written at every one, are the test we most lack, and on passages stage 9 supports only an evaluation warning. (ii)~The confirmation and the window use one story template, and the window three 7--8B models (the trained remap two, with one seed); its depth differs between models, and we claim no universal depth. (iii)~A later mention here repeats the word form (plurals open \GfourteenPluralMin--\GfourteenPluralMax{} of the read; discovery sample); paraphrases and pronouns are untested. (iv)~A list before the writing token gives a small, unexplained negative key read (to $\idKBeforeQwenSeventytwo$ nats at Qwen2.5-72B). (v)~The readers' duplicate-token core depends on how many heads are counted, ablation and knockout disagree on whether the copy route takes over (\cref{app:heads}), and how the answer prefers the moved-to location over the initial one is unresolved. (vi)~The mechanism was tested in two 7B models (and at 1.5B and 3B), the channel-ratio law in three 7--8B models, all in BF16; eager attention, which Gemma-2 requires, lowers the key read by 12--17\,\% in one model (exploratory), though no Gemma-2-9B verdict lies within 0.03 of its threshold; the list-after share falls beyond 14B for unknown reasons (discovery sample). % verbatim: "12 % (AFTER) and 17 % (POST)" and "no Gemma-2-9B verdict is within 0.03 of its threshold", outcome J part B
(vii)~Values span one to five tokens and prompts are English; the models of the final round and of stage 9 are instruction-tuned, and in the base models we ran on IOI a later list opened no positive key read (a weak null; discovery sample, exploratory; \cref{app:g-ioi}). (viii)~We re-tested no third-party channel claim with and without a later mention.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
Across seven model families on fresh stories, and for an appended options list on counterfactual passages in every evaluable model, later mentions decide whether a written value is looked up through its key or copied from its value; on fresh stories the decision reaches generated answers. % seven = \JxFamiliesABCD (design count)
In two 7B models the matching heads write a flag that the answer reads by key, sufficient but neither specific nor the only carrier. Where the candidates are named also sets when an edit can act: with them listed after the story, patches and, in all but one cell, single-site edits of the writing token act only through its early layers, up to a model-specific depth, and fail silently beyond it; steering at every position escapes with a named list. A preregistered law predicting an edit's channels from the model's read failed.
