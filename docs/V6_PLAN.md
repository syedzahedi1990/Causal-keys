## Stage 9 in one paragraph (what the paper must now say)

Stage 9's predictions were frozen in a dated note before the run. It is not a preregistration entry and has no class
priors. The design was 150 new fresh stories (N9), 80 edit cores (E9) and stage 8a's 185 counterfactual SQuAD passages,
in Mistral-7B, Llama-3.1-8B and Qwen2.5-7B.

- **The patches.** The *late* patch overwrites the writing token's cached key and value with the counterfactual (S)
  run's from block l to the top. The *early* patch does it only below l.
- **The finding.** Whether the candidates are listed after the story sets *when* the token can still change the
  answer.
  - **Free form.** A late patch changes every answer until 69-93 % of the depth: l_c(NONE)/L = 22/32, 26/32, 26/28.
  - **Listed after (AFTER, LETTER).** The late window closes at 19-50 % of the depth.
  - **Size.** The window shrinks by 0.43-0.50 L (P2 SUPPORTED in all three models).
  - **Position, not presence.** The same list placed BEFORE the story keeps the window open, so it is the list's
    position, not its presence (F2s not triggered).
- **The failure is silent.** Where the late patch fails in AFTER, 98.9-100 % of answers are the stale answer, with
  median p 0.998 at Mistral (P4 SUPPORTED).
- **The crossover (P3s supported at Llama and Qwen, not at Mistral).** The early patch moves 0.00 of free-form answers
  in every model, and 0.64-0.98 of AFTER and LETTER answers.
- **Passages.**
  - At Mistral and Llama, an appended options list closes the late window (OPTA 0.08 and 0.28 against OPTB 0.99 and
    0.95), and the early window opens (OPTA 0.80 and 0.52).
  - At Qwen the options leave it open (OPTA 0.94), so F2 triggers.
  - Pre-written reading: the passage result is reported as an evaluation warning only, with no mechanism claim on
    passages.
- **Edits.**
  - Single-site steering (E1) tracks the natural patch in 47 of 48 cells (P5a NOT SUPPORTED: one cell at Qwen,
    -0.106).
  - The trained DAS remap (E4b) changes 0.64-1.00 of free-form answers, and its transfer to AFTER falls to 0.00-0.30
    beyond block 3 (P5b NOT SUPPORTED: at Mistral, block 3 has tau 0.703 against the 0.7 cut).
  - All-position steering (CAA) transfers, 0.96-1.00 where the patch's AFTER flip is 0.00-0.01. Pre-written reading:
    the window claim is stated for single-site edits, patching and KV-cache edits only.
- **The key share predicts transfer.** r_K (the edit's key projection relative to the natural key displacement)
  predicts whether an edit transfers to AFTER, with AUC 0.90-0.94 in all three models (B3 SUPPORTED).
- **Dose.**
  - Half-dose edits move only 0.31-0.47 of free-form answers (B1, B2 NOT SUPPORTED).
  - Pre-written reading: describe the convexity without a threshold claim. The failed channel-ratio law of stage 8
    stays reported as failed.
- **Totals.**
  - 3 SUPPORTED, 7 NOT SUPPORTED, 0 NOT EVALUABLE over P1-P4, P5a, P5b, POST and B1-B3.
  - P1 fails because Llama's OPTB passages (options before) close below 0.8 at x = 0.45: the late flip at block 14 is 0.70.
  - POST fails because Mistral's neutral sentence after the story partly closes the window (late flip 0.73-0.88 from
    0.25 L).
