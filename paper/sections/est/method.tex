\section{Setup}\label{sec:method}

\paragraph{Stories, runs and formats.}
We use the belief stories of \citet{anonymous2026fitted} (``[\dots] \{agent\} watches as the \{$o$\} is moved to the \{\textsc{loc}\}. [\dots] Where does \{agent\} believe the \{$o$\} is?''; \cref{app:prompts}). Base and source stories B and S differ only at the writing token \{\textsc{loc}\}, at position $p$; a third value X is absent from the story. \fmtOpt{} lists the candidates after the question (``Answer with exactly one choice''), \fmtLetter{} labels them A--F; under ``Answer with one word'', \fmtListA{} lists them there, \fmtPost{} names them in a neutral sentence after the story, \fmtNull{} replaces it by one of the same length that names none, \fmtSentB{} and \fmtBefore{} put the sentence or the list before the story, and \fmtNone{} names none (\emph{free form}). A \emph{later mention} is a token after $p$ that repeats a candidate's word form, up to case. The \emph{fresh stories} are 150 new cores, with eight new mention sentences, a second location lexicon and per-core candidate orders (\cref{app:fresh}); stage 9 draws \NqStories{} more.

\paragraph{Passages.}
From the SQuAD v1.1 development set \citep{rajpurkar2016squad} we take questions whose answer is a person, place or number named exactly once in the passage, with no content word of it elsewhere, and write a counterfactual entity in its place \citep{longpre2021conflicts}: 185 items from 34 articles, with answers of one to five tokens, clamped at every position of the span. Formats: options after the question (by name or letter) or before the passage, a related-articles sentence after or before it, or free form. We score the first answer token (\cref{app:natural}).

\paragraph{Clamps and patches.}
$\mathcal{C}_K(Z)$ sets the writing token's pre-RoPE key in every layer from $\ell_0$ ($0$ unless stated) to that of run $Z$, and $\mathcal{C}_V(Z)$ its value. Later tokens see the writing token only through these, so clamping both from layer~0 reproduces run $Z$ exactly (checked in FP32; \cref{app:impl}). A single-channel clamp creates a cache state that no prompt produces; generated answers under it show that the read reaches behaviour, not that the state is natural. The \emph{late patch} at onset $l$ clamps both channels to S's from $\ell_0 = l$ to the top layer, the \emph{early patch} only in the layers below $l$; together they are the full patch, which reproduces S.

\paragraph{Measures.}
With $\Delta_Y(Z)$ the change in $\log p(Y)$ at the answer under $\mathcal{C}_K(Z)$ against a self-clamp row in the same batch,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:idk}
\mathrm{ID}_K = \tfrac{1}{2}\big[\Delta_{\mathrm{S}}(\mathrm{S}) - \Delta_{\mathrm{S}}(\mathrm{X}) + \Delta_{\mathrm{X}}(\mathrm{X}) - \Delta_{\mathrm{X}}(\mathrm{S})\big],
\end{equation}
that is, how much more a key clamp to S favours S over X than a clamp to X does, averaged symmetrically; effects shared by all foreign keys, and a prior for B, cancel, and it is zero when the key carries no identity. $\mathrm{ID}_V$ is defined likewise with values, $s_{\mathrm{ID}} = \overline{\mathrm{ID}}_K/(\overline{\mathrm{ID}}_K + \overline{\mathrm{ID}}_V)$ is a ratio of means, and $r$ is a format's $\overline{\mathrm{ID}}_K$ relative to that with the list after the story. Fresh stories are scored on the forms each model emits (the \emph{emitted-form score}; \cref{app:emitted}). Under each clamp we also decode greedily: $\beta_K$ ($\beta_V$) is the share of answers that move to the clamped value under the key-only (value-only) clamp, net of moves to it under the clamp to the other of S and X (the flip rate omits this).

\paragraph{Models and statistics.}
The discovery sample has \nModels{} models: Qwen2.5 at 1.5B--72B \citep{yang2024qwen25}, Qwen3-8B \citep{yang2025qwen3}, Mistral-7B \citep{jiang2023mistral}, Mistral-Small-24B \citep{mistral2025small} and OLMo-2-7B \citep{olmo2025two}; the final round adds Llama-3.1-8B \citep{grattafiori2024llama3}, Gemma-2-9B and Gemma-2-2B \citep{gemma2024gemma2}, Phi-4 \citep{abdin2024phi4} and Falcon3-7B \citep{tii2024falcon3}; stage 9 uses Mistral-7B, Llama-3.1-8B and Qwen2.5-7B. All are instruction-tuned and run in BF16, those of the final round from official weights (sha256-verified; \cref{app:compute}). Intervals are 95\,\% percentile bootstrap intervals, two-stage over clusters on fresh stories, passages and in stage 9, and over stories in the mechanism tests, with the fitted flag and the frozen heads held fixed (so they omit the fit's uncertainty); the channel-ratio law's statistics carry its preregistered 90\,\% hierarchical intervals (\cref{app:law}). A range over models gives the smallest and largest estimate.

\paragraph{Preregistration, and what we report.}
The planned analyses of stages 1--8 were preregistered in ten entries, each committed before its run, with its scoring code fixed before any output was inspected; those of stage 9 were frozen in a dated note before its run (\cref{app:prereg-how,app:s9-note}). Each prediction of the final round had a recorded probability of holding (\emph{risky} if below 0.8; \cref{tab:status}) and, if primary, a pre-written sentence for each outcome; the note pre-wrote such readings without probabilities, and where one restricts a claim we follow it. Most predictions compared an estimate with a preset threshold; the main text reports the estimates. In the final round and stage 9 a result was planned unless labelled exploratory or post hoc; results labelled discovery sample come from the earlier entries. The appendix gives every verdict, including the many failures: of the final round's risky predictions, \JxRiskyMetABCD{} of the \JxRiskyVerdABCD{} with a verdict held, fewer than the \JxRiskyExpABCD{} expected, though within chance if the lines are treated as independent (post hoc), and \JxRiskyNotEvalABCD{} could not be evaluated; \NqTotSupported{} of the \NqTotLines{} lines of stage 9's note were supported. \Cref{tab:status} gives all tallies, \cref{app:failures-j} every failure of the final round, \cref{app:prereg-ai} those of the earlier entries, among them a transfer law for interventions and a key-share threshold, \cref{app:stage9} every stage-9 verdict, and \cref{app:crosswalk} maps each claim to its prediction.
