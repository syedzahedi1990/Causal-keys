\section{Where an intervention appears to act}\label{sec:intervention}

\citet{anonymous2026fitted} read their key exchange between M and P as causal evidence that keys induced by the learned patch contribute to the behavioural difference between the two, under their tested multilayer intervention and the prompt format we call \fmtOpt{}. Our runs of their released bases reproduce their saved answers under \fmtOpt{} (preregistered gate; \cref{sec:method}). We then change only the format and add a value-only exchange. Two comparisons are confirmatory and met in both models: C3, $\psi_K(\fmtNone{}) < 0.5\,\psi_K(\fmtOpt{})$, and E4, $\psi_V(\fmtNone{}) \geq 0.5$ and $> \psi_K(\fmtNone{})$; the rest of this paragraph and the next is exploratory (\cref{tab:frames}).

\paragraph{Behaviour barely changes across formats; the carrying channel flips.}
$\varphi$ lies between \phiOptMistral{} and \phiLetterMistral{} at Mistral-Small-24B and between \phiNoneQwen{} and \phiBeforeQwen{} at Qwen2.5-72B, and the PCA control transfers the source location in every format. Yet at Qwen2.5-72B, M's keys reproduce \psiLetterQwen{} of the M$-$P difference under \fmtLetter{} and \psiOptQwen{} under \fmtOpt{}, but only \psiPostQwen{} under \fmtPost{}, \psiNoneQwen{} under \fmtNone{} and $\psiBeforeQwen$ under \fmtBefore{}, while M's values do the reverse ($\psi_V$ = \psiVLetterQwen{}, \psiVOptQwen{}, \psiVPostQwen{}, \psiVNoneQwen{}, \psiVBeforeQwen{}). Mistral-Small-24B and the removal fractions $\rho_K$ and $\rho_V$ show the same crossover. Across the ten format--model cells, the remap's key share $\psi_K/(\psi_K+\psi_V)$ tracks the identity key share $s_{\mathrm{ID}}$ of the unpatched model in the same format, measured on our stories at the same pinned revisions (\cref{fig:frames}c; $r = \crossR{}$; largest gap under \fmtPost{}).

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
In \fmtLetter{}, \fmtOpt{}, \fmtPost{} and \fmtNone{} the prompt is identical up to the end of the story, so under the causal mask M writes the same key and the same value at the writing token in all four; only their readers differ. The edit is therefore present in both channels, and which one appears to carry it depends on which channel the format's readers use. This reading was formed after the data of preregistration C; its value half (E4) and the refit test below (F) were then preregistered and confirmed. We localised the readers only at 1.5--14B (\cref{sec:readers}), so that they are the same at 24B and 72B is an inference. The addition fractions sum to less than one and the removal fractions mostly to more than one (\cref{tab:frames}), so the channels do not combine additively, and part of the edit may travel through the other patched span tokens. In their format we reproduce their key result: M's keys alone reproduce a substantial part of the M$-$P difference in both models ($\psi_K$ = \psiOptMistral{} and \psiOptQwen{}) and, in a comparison they did not make, more of it than M's values do. They scoped their claim to their tested setting; that setting must include the prompt, because which channel carries the intervention depends on the prompt it is measured under. We did not re-test their colour-lookup questions, and we score log-odds fractions where they scored argmax agreement.

\subsection{A remap fit without later mentions}\label{sec:refit}

If the readers decide, a remap fit where nothing later reads the writing token's key should still show the crossover; if instead the fitting format decides what the remap writes, a remap fit under \fmtNone{} should stay value-carried with lettered options. Preregistration F tested this at Mistral-Small-24B. We ran the released training code of \citet{anonymous2026fitted} unmodified, changing only the prompt: under \fmtNone{} (fit\_none) and, as a same-code control, under their own format (fit\_p1), three seeds each, each paired with its own PCA control (\cref{app:refit}). All gates were met: the refits start from the released PCA subspace, they work under their fitting prompts ($\varphi = \phiNoneRefNone{}$ and $\phiOptRefOpt{}$), and the control reproduces the released crossover. All four predictions were met (\cref{tab:refit}): fit\_none is carried by keys under \fmtLetter{} ($\psi_K = \psiLetterRefNone{}$, $\psi_V = \psiVLetterRefNone{}$) and by values under \fmtNone{} ($\psi_V = \psiVNoneRefNone{}$); it transfers to lettered options ($\varphi = \phiLetterRefNone{}$); its crossover equals the control's (difference in differences $\refD{}$ [$\refDLo{}$, $\refDHi{}$]); and its key share tracks the natural read ($r = \refRNone{}$; \cref{fig:frames}c). The three remaps agree within \refMaxDiff{} in $\psi_K$ and $\psi_V$ in every format, although fit\_none's subspace overlaps the released remap's no more than two seeds of one fit overlap each other. The fitting format changes how well the remap transfers (exploratory: fit\_none reaches $\varphi = \phiNoneRefNone{}$ under \fmtNone{} and \phiLetterRefNone{} under \fmtLetter{}, fit\_p1 \phiNoneRefOpt{} and \phiLetterRefOpt{}), but not which channel appears to carry it.

\subsection{Preregistered predictions that failed}\label{sec:failed}

A preregistered transfer law predicted that without a later mention the remap transfers only as far as the value channel carries it: $\varphi(\fmtNone{})$ within $\pm 0.15$ of a value-channel completeness $\kappa_V$ estimated beforehand by clamping (C1), and $\varphi(\fmtOpt{}) > \varphi(\fmtNone{})$ (C2). C1 fails at Mistral-Small-24B ($\varphi = \phiNoneMistral{}$ [\phiLoNoneMistral{}, \phiHiNoneMistral{}] against $\kappa_V = 0.53$) and holds only at the edge of its margin at Qwen2.5-72B; C2 is null at Mistral-Small-24B and small at Qwen2.5-72B ($+0.034$ [$+0.018$, $+0.049$]). A key-share threshold $s_K \geq 0.80$ under \fmtOpt{} (C4) held only at Qwen2.5-32B of the three large models (\cref{fig:scale}), while its \fmtBefore{} control held in all three (\cref{app:prereg}).
