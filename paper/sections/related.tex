\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Options, belief stories and heads.}
Heads that select listed options \citep{lieberum2023chinchilla,wiegreffe2025answer}, some by query--key alignment \citep{tulchinskii2024wise}, correspond to our second hop; closest to our first, shallower heads write into option rows a routing feature that redirects decision heads' attention \citep{sun2026persuaded}, the role of our flag. In multiple-choice answering the winning option is decodable at its content right after the last option, before it is bound to a symbol \citep{wong2026decide}; our early window fits a decision made at the listed options. On belief stories, belief and reality share a value slot read out under a router at the query \citep{steele2026routing}, and lookbacks built on ordering IDs retrieve the state token to track beliefs \citep{prakash2025lookbacks}; in our terms both fit a copy route, and later mentions add a lookup addressed at the mention.

\paragraph{Patching, cache edits and steering.}
Activation patching results depend on the metric and the corruption method \citep{zhang2023patching} and need care in interpretation \citep{heimersheim2024patching}; the format matters too: with a later list, a patch at a context token stops changing the answer beyond a model-specific depth. Overwriting a field's cached key and value while reusing the rest of the cache can leave the model acting on the old value \citep{bojieli2026notes}, and contrastive changes to cached values can steer behaviour \citep{pustovit2026packs}; our patches recompute every later token, yet a late overwrite still fails once the candidates are named after the field. Interchange interventions \citep{geiger2021inducing,geiger2024finding} may act through pathways the model does not use naturally (\citealp{makelov2024subspace}, contested by \citealp{wu2024reply}; \citealp{liu2026wrong}); steering vectors \citep{turner2023actadd,panickssery2023caa} need not reproduce natural activations \citep{shih2026steering} and are brittle to prompt changes \citep{tan2024steering}, and a steering gain under one answer encoding does not identify what the intervention controls \citep{gao2026encodings}; prompt format changes what is measured \citep{sclar2023quantifying,opielka2026causality}. Cache-level accounts place routing or binding information in keys \citep{ma2025addressbook,oh2026rebinding} or read an operation's cache mainly as an address \citep{wu2026record}; in our stories a written value is read mainly through its key only when a later token re-mentions the candidates.
