\paragraph{What to do differently.}
(i)~State where the candidates are mentioned relative to the token that writes a value, and test channel claims with and without a later mention. (ii)~Check localisation methods \citep{mueller2025mib} in the format of the behaviour they should explain. (iii)~Do not infer an intervention's channel from the model's own read: even near-natural steering vectors were under-read by the keys except at the shallowest depth (exploratory), and the remap we trained was read mainly through its value. Measure its channels with key-only and value-only exchanges, and report its distance from the natural state (post hoc here).

\paragraph{How the predictions fared.}
The risky predictions (\cref{tab:status}) held \JxRiskyMetABCD{} of \JxRiskyVerdABCD{} against \JxRiskyExpABCD{} expected, within chance under the recorded priors (probability \JxRiskyTailP{} of so few or fewer if the predictions are independent; post hoc). Every extrapolation with a verdict on claims 1 and 2 held (probability \JxClassMTailPABD{} that all would); those on the intervention law held \JcClassMMet{} of \JcClassMLines{} against \JcClassMExp{} expected (probability \JcClassMTailP{} if independent, which overstates the evidence: two of them test the same law, and one failed through its control), the one class whose priors were too high. In entries G to I, \GHIcountFailed{} of \GHIcountVerd{} predictions with a verdict failed, and in entries A to F a transfer law for interventions and a key-share threshold failed (\cref{app:prereg-ai}); every failure is listed in \cref{app:failures-j}.

\paragraph{Limitations.}\label{sec:limitations}
(i)~Our passages are counterfactual SQuAD items with added options and the answer named once, scored at the first answer token. Passages in which the answer recurs later, with the counterfactual written at every mention, are the test we most lack. (ii)~The confirmation uses one story template. (iii)~A list before the writing token gives a small negative key read (to $\idKBeforeQwenSeventytwo$ nats at Qwen2.5-72B), as in IOI, where in-sentence re-mentions are read negatively too: perhaps a suppression of a word seen twice, by heads we did not identify. (iv)~The read is not in the lowest layers' keys (at Qwen2.5-1.5B it lies in layers 4--15 of 28; exploratory), but with the clamp started at a third of the depth much of it disappears (on passages with options after, \JaEoneOnOptaMin--\JaEoneOnOptaMax{} remains; exploratory). (v)~The readers' duplicate-token core depends on how many heads are counted; ablation and knockout disagree on whether the copy route takes over; how the answer prefers the moved-to location over the initial one, also mentioned and matched, is unresolved. (vi)~The mechanism was tested in two 7B models (and at 1.5B and 3B), the interventions in three 7--8B models, all in BF16; eager attention, which Gemma-2 requires, lowers the key read by 12--17\,\% in one model (exploratory), though no Gemma-2-9B verdict lies within 0.03 of its threshold. % verbatim: "12 % (AFTER) and 17 % (POST)" and "no Gemma-2-9B verdict is within 0.03 of its threshold", outcome J part B
(vii)~Values span one to four tokens; prompts are English; all models are instruction-tuned and prompted in their chat templates (in the one base model we ran, on IOI, a later list opened no key read, a weak null; exploratory). (viii)~We re-tested no third-party channel claim with and without a later mention.

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
Across seven model families on fresh stories, and for an appended options list on counterfactual reading-comprehension passages in every evaluable model, later mentions decide whether a written value is looked up through its key or copied from its value; on fresh stories the decision reaches generated answers. % seven = \JxFamiliesABCD (design count)
In two 7B models the lookup is a duplicate-token match that writes a flag the answer reads by key, sufficient but neither specific nor the only carrier. Where an intervention appears to act depends on the prompt and on the edit; a preregistered law predicting it from the model's own read failed.
