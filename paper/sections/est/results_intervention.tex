\textbf{With the candidates listed after the story, overwriting the writing token's cache changes most answers only from early layers, up to a model-specific depth, although it changes free-form answers until most of the depth.} Single-site edits largely follow it (\cref{tab:estimates}).

\paragraph{Design.}
Stage 9 ran Mistral-7B, Llama-3.1-8B and Qwen2.5-7B on \NqStories{} new stories, \NqCores{} new edit cores and the \NqPassages{} passages, scoring generated answers (\cref{app:stage9}). The late and early patches (\cref{sec:method}) are applied at every second onset $l$; the \emph{flip} is the share of answers that change to S's, and \emph{listed} answers are those under \fmtListA{} and \fmtLetter. The late window closes at $l_c$, the first onset whose late patch flips fewer than half of the answers. The onset $l_b$ is the first whose late patch flips at most 0.2 of \fmtListA{} answers on \NqCalib{} earlier stories, fixed before the new ones were scored. The dated note fixed the analyses below, unless labelled post hoc, with a prediction for most (the inconsistency of free-form and listed answers and all-position steering are reported without one); the last paragraph returns to the final round. None of the note's pre-written readings fits the window (\cref{app:s9-note}), so our wording of it is post hoc.

\paragraph{The late window.}
Without a later mention, the late patch changes at least \NqPoneMinMin{} of the answers on stories from every onset up to half the depth (\fmtNone, \fmtBefore, \fmtNull{}; lowest at Llama-3.1-8B, \ci{\NqPoneMinLlamaEight}{\NqPoneMinLlamaEightLo}{\NqPoneMinLlamaEightHi}), and under \fmtNone{} the window closes only at $l_c/L$ = \NqLcNoneMistralSeven/\NqLayersMistralSeven, \NqLcNoneLlamaEight/\NqLayersLlamaEight{} and \NqLcNoneQwenSeven/\NqLayersQwenSeven{} (\cref{fig:window}a--c). With the candidates listed after the story, as words or letters, it closes at $l_c/L$ = \NqLcFracListedMin--\NqLcFracListedMax; under \fmtListA{} that is \NqGapNoneAfterMin--\NqGapNoneAfterMax{} of the depth earlier than under \fmtNone{} (lower 95\,\% bounds at least \NqGapNoneAfterMistralSevenLo). The list's position matters more than its presence: with the list before the story the window closes at \NqLcFracBeforeMin--\NqLcFracBeforeMax, \NqGapBeforeAfterMin--\NqGapBeforeAfterMax{} of the depth later than with it after, though earlier than with no mention (post hoc). The full patch changes \NqFullMin--\NqFullMax{} of the answers in every format, so listed answers are not sticky.

\paragraph{Late patches fail silently.}
With onset $l_b$, the late patch changes only \NqLateListedMin--\NqLateListedMax{} of listed answers, against \NqLateFreeMin{} without a later mention. Of the \fmtListA{} answers it does not change, \pctn{\NqSilentShareAfterMin}--\pct{\NqSilentShareAfterMax} are the old answer B, with median probability \NqSilentPbAfterMin--\NqSilentPbAfterMax{} (predicted at Mistral-7B only). In \pctn{\NqInconsMin}--\pct{\NqInconsMax} of stories the patch changes the free-form answer and leaves the listed one (lowest at Mistral-7B, \ci{\NqInconsMistralSeven}{\NqInconsMistralSevenLo}{\NqInconsMistralSevenHi}): an edit validated in free form would pass while deciding nothing in the listed format.

\paragraph{The early window.}
Patching only the layers below $l_b$ changes \NqEarlyListedMin--\NqEarlyListedMax{} of listed answers and \NqEarlyFreeMax{} without a later mention (\cref{fig:window}, dashed). The listed share is lowest at Mistral-7B (\fmtListA{} \ci{\NqEarlyAfterMistralSeven}{\NqEarlyAfterMistralSevenLo}{\NqEarlyAfterMistralSevenHi}, \fmtLetter{} \ci{\NqEarlyLetterMistralSeven}{\NqEarlyLetterMistralSevenLo}{\NqEarlyLetterMistralSevenHi}), where the late patch from $l_b$ still changes \NqLateAfterMistralSeven{} of \fmtListA{} answers. Post hoc, and untested here, this fits \cref{sec:claim2}: with a list the value is read early, at its later mention.

\paragraph{Scope.}
A neutral sentence naming the candidates after the story keeps the late patch effective from $l_b$ to half the depth at Qwen2.5-7B (\NqPostLateFromLbMinQwenSeven) and largely at Llama-3.1-8B (\NqPostLateFromLbMinLlamaEight--\NqPostLateFromLbMaxLlamaEight), but less fully at Mistral-7B (\NqPostLateFromLbMinMistralSeven--\NqPostLateFromLbMaxMistralSeven). On passages, whether options after the passage close the late window depended on the model: they did at Mistral-7B and Llama-3.1-8B, not at Qwen2.5-7B. Following the note's pre-written reading, we report this only as an evaluation warning (whether an intervention changes the answer can depend on the answer format), with no mechanism claim (\cref{app:s9-results}). Two passage cells also differ from stories: with options before the passage, Llama-3.1-8B's late patch at $0.45L$ changes only \ci{\NqPsgLateOptbFortyfiveLlamaEight}{\NqPsgLateOptbFortyfiveLlamaEightLo}{\NqPsgLateOptbFortyfiveLlamaEightHi} of answers (on stories without a later mention, at least \NqPoneMinMin), and at Qwen2.5-7B the early patch up to $0.3L$ with options after changes none (\NqPsgEarlyOptaThirtyQwenSeven).

\paragraph{Single-site edits largely inherit the window.}
A single-site steering vector written at the writing token (E1, the difference of its mean residual between stories with the two values; \citealp{turner2023actadd,panickssery2023caa}) changes about as many answers as the natural patch at the same block (\cref{fig:window}d); over the \NqEoneCellsAll{} format--block cells its largest departure in each model is a shortfall, $\NqEoneGapMistralSeven$, $\NqEoneGapLlamaEight$ and $\NqEoneGapQwenSeven$ (Qwen2.5-7B, \fmtListA, block 11), each interval below zero, to $\NqEoneGapQwenSevenLo$ at Qwen2.5-7B (\cref{tab:estimates}). A rank-16 subspace remap trained there without a later mention, with the recipe of \citet{anonymous2026fitted} (E4b; Mistral-7B and Qwen2.5-7B), changes \NqEfourbNoneMin--\NqEfourbNoneMax{} of free-form answers but transfers to \fmtListA{} only in part: the ratio $\tau$ of its \fmtListA{} to its free-form flips is \NqTauEfourbThreeMistralSeven{} and \NqTauEfourbThreeQwenSeven{} at block 3, where the natural patch's is \NqTauNatThreeMistralSeven{} in both, and \NqTauEfourbBeyondMin--\NqTauEfourbBeyondMax{} beyond. Steering at every position from the writing token on, generated tokens included (CAA; \citealp{panickssery2023caa}), differs: at blocks 11 and 15, where the natural patch changes \NqPfivecNatReadMin--\NqPfivecNatReadMax{} of \fmtListA{} answers (Qwen2.5-7B: block 15 only), it changes \NqPfivecCaaMin--\NqPfivecCaaMax{} of them, but only \NqEditCaaLetterFifteenQwenSeven--\NqEditCaaLetterFifteenMistralSeven{} of \fmtLetter{} answers at block 15, and at block 3 fewer of these than the natural patch (\cref{app:s9-results}). As it also writes at the later mentions and the answer, the window claim is for single-site edits, patching and KV-cache edits only.

\paragraph{Inside the window, the delivered key share predicts transfer.}
An edit's \emph{delivered key share} $r_K$ is the projection of its displacement of the writing token's keys (all heads, layers above the edit) on the natural displacement, divided by the latter's squared norm ($r_K = 1$ for the natural patch). Among edits that change the free-form answer, written where the natural patch still changes at least 0.9 of \fmtListA{} answers (block 3, and 7 at Qwen2.5-7B; steering vectors at four doses, the remap, and cache interpolations, whose $r_K$ is their weight), $r_K$ predicts whether the \fmtListA{} answer changes too, with AUC \NqAucMin--\NqAucMax{} (\cref{tab:estimates}). Beyond the window it cannot help: the natural patch itself fails there. Dose matters steeply. At block 3, half-dose edits change \NqHalfNoneMin--\NqHalfNoneMax{} of free-form answers and \NqHalfAfterMin--\NqHalfAfterMax{} of \fmtListA{} ones; interpolating the cache with weight 0.65 changes \NqDoseJSixtyfiveNoneThreeLlamaEight--\NqDoseJSixtyfiveNoneThreeQwenSeven{} and \NqDoseJSixtyfiveAfterThreeQwenSeven--\NqDoseJSixtyfiveAfterThreeLlamaEight{} of them. The response is convex in both formats and rises later with the list (post hoc); we claim no threshold.

\paragraph{Edits do not keep the model's own channel ratio.}
The final round's law concerned which channel carries an edit, not whether it reaches the answer: one written at depth $l$ would be read through key and value in the model's own ratio there, $\lambda = \log(\psi_K/\psi_V) \approx 0$ ($\psi_C$: its identity effect through channel $C$ relative to the natural write's; \cref{app:law-brief}). Even steering vectors close to the natural edit depart from the natural channel ratio; the format law describes the model's own written value and does not predict how an edit is attributed. % verbatim: C-12 outcome (e), entry J part C
Pooled over depths, the keys under-read the steering vectors by 4.5--12\,\% in the three models ($\bar\lambda$ from $\JcTwoPoolEoneLlamaEight$ to $\JcTwoPoolEtwoLlamaEight$), every pooled value and its 90\,\% interval below zero but inside the preregistered margin of $\pm\log 1.25$; single statistics cross the margin, one wholly outside it. Relative to the model's ratio, a learned remap is read far more through the value: $\bar\lambda$ = \ci{$\JcFourPoolEfourQwenSeven$}{$\JcFourPoolEfourQwenSevenLo$}{$\JcFourPoolEfourQwenSevenHi$} and \ci{$\JcFourPoolEfourMistralSeven$}{$\JcFourPoolEfourMistralSevenLo$}{$\JcFourPoolEfourMistralSevenHi$} at Qwen2.5-7B and Mistral-7B, growing with depth (at block 7 after a sentence $\psi_K$ = \JcFourPsiKWpostSevenQwenSeven{} and \JcFourPsiKWpostSevenMistralSeven, $\psi_V$ = \JcFourPsiVWpostSevenQwenSeven{} and \JcFourPsiVWpostSevenMistralSeven; post hoc).
