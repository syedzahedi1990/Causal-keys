\section{Related Work}\label{sec:related}

\paragraph{Where attention looks and what it moves.}
Every head factors into a QK circuit, which computes where it attends, and an OV circuit, which computes what the attended tokens contribute \citep{elhage2021framework}; attention weights alone do not determine a head's output \citep{kobayashi2020norm}. Duplicate-token heads attend to earlier copies of the current token, and induction heads to the token that followed such a copy \citep{wang2022ioi,olsson2022context}; induction can copy tokens or whole concepts \citep{feucht2025dualroute}, a copy-suppression head suppresses earlier occurrences of a predicted token \citep{mcdougall2023copy}, and circuit components are reused across tasks \citep{merullo2024reuse}. Our first hop is a duplicate-token match; we ask which cache channel it opens, what it writes and what sets the sign of its use.

\paragraph{Binding, options and position.}
Bound entities are retrieved through binding IDs \citep{feng2023binding}, an ordering subspace \citep{dai2024binding}, position \citep{prakash2024finetuning}, lookbacks \citep{prakash2025lookbacks} or a mix of positional, lexical and reflexive mechanisms \citep{gurarieh2025mixing}; belief and reality share a value slot read out under a router at the query \citep{steele2026routing}. Specific heads select among options \citep{lieberum2023chinchilla,wiegreffe2025answer}, a winner is chosen before it is bound to a symbol \citep{wong2026decide}, and middle-layer heads select by query--key alignment \citep{tulchinskii2024wise}, which our second hop resembles; options placed before a context cannot attend to it \citep{ok2026lost}. We separate the two cache channels of the token that states a value and show that the placement of the candidates decides which one carries its identity, under a free-answer instruction and on passages; which of the mechanisms of \citet{gurarieh2025mixing} our lookup corresponds to, if any, we do not test.

\paragraph{Attributing interventions.}
Interchange interventions \citep{geiger2021inducing,geiger2024finding} may act through pathways the model does not use naturally (\citealp{makelov2024subspace,liu2026wrong}; contested by \citealp{wu2024reply}) and need not reproduce natural activations \citep{shih2026steering}; steering vectors \citep{turner2023actadd,panickssery2023caa} are brittle to prompt changes \citep{tan2024steering}, and prompt format changes what is measured \citep{sclar2023quantifying,opielka2026causality}. Cache-level accounts place routing or binding information in keys \citep{ma2025addressbook,oh2026rebinding} or find an operation's cache read mainly as an address \citep{wu2026record}. We find that the channel credited with an edit of a written value depends on the prompt and on the edit, and that a preregistered law predicting it from the model's own read fails.
