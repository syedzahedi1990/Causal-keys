\section{Where an intervention appears to act}\label{sec:intervention}

\citet{anonymous2026fitted} read their key exchange between M and P, under \fmtOpt{}, as causal evidence that keys induced by the learned patch contribute to the behavioural difference between the two; we reproduce their result (\cref{app:discussion}). We then change only the format and add a value-only exchange. Two comparisons are confirmatory and met in both models: without a later mention M's keys carry less than half of what they carry under \fmtOpt{} (C3), and M's values carry most of the effect (E4); the rest is exploratory (\cref{tab:frames}).

\paragraph{Behaviour barely changes across formats; the carrying channel flips.}
$\varphi$ lies between \phiOptMistral{} and \phiLetterMistral{} at Mistral-Small-24B and between \phiNoneQwen{} and \phiBeforeQwen{} at Qwen2.5-72B, and the PCA control transfers the source location in every format. Yet at Qwen2.5-72B, M's keys reproduce \psiLetterQwen{} of the M$-$P difference under \fmtLetter{} but \psiNoneQwen{} under \fmtNone{}, while M's values do the reverse (\cref{fig:frames}b). Mistral-Small-24B and the removal fractions (\cref{app:impl}) show the same crossover. Across the ten format--model cells, the remap's key share $\psi_K/(\psi_K+\psi_V)$ tracks the identity key share $s_{\mathrm{ID}}$ of the unpatched model in the same format (\cref{fig:frames}c; $r = \crossR{}$). This is post hoc here, preregistered for the refits below, and failed out of sample for a binding edit (\cref{sec:prakash}).

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
In \fmtLetter{}, \fmtOpt{}, \fmtPost{} and \fmtNone{} the prompt is identical up to the end of the story, so under the causal mask M writes the same key and the same value at the writing token in all four; only their readers differ, and which channel appears to carry the edit follows them. This reading is post hoc (C); its value half (E4) and the refit test below (F) were then preregistered and confirmed. It concerns edits that change which value is written; on the material of \citet{prakash2025lookbacks} we show it only for an identity edit at block 0 (\cref{sec:prakash}). That the readers are the same at 24B and 72B is an inference.

\subsection{A remap fit without later mentions}\label{sec:refit}

The remap edits block 4, upstream of every exchanged key and value. Preregistration F asked whether the format it was fit in, rather than its readers, decides its channel: at Mistral-Small-24B it refit the remap with the released code under \fmtNone{} (fit\_none) and, as a same-code control, under \fmtOpt{} (fit\_opt; \cref{app:refit}). All gates and all four predictions were met (\cref{tab:prereg-f,tab:refit}). fit\_none is carried by keys under \fmtLetter{} ($\psi_K = \psiLetterRefNone{}$) and by values under \fmtNone{} ($\psi_V = \psiVNoneRefNone{}$), transfers to lettered options ($\varphi = \phiLetterRefNone{}$), shows the control's crossover (difference in differences $\refD{}$ [$\refDLo{}$, $\refDHi{}$]), and its key share tracks the natural read ($r = \refRNone{}$; \cref{fig:frames}c). Post hoc, the fitting format still lowers fit\_none's $\psi_K$ under \fmtOpt{} and \fmtPost{} (by \refDiffOpt{} and \refDiffPost{}). The reading format decides which channel dominates.

\subsection{A binding edit from another lab}\label{sec:prakash}

Preregistration H applied the exchange to an edit we did not design: the state-token patch BIND of \citet{prakash2025lookbacks}, at Qwen2.5-14B on their material, with an identity edit ID as a control (\cref{tab:prakash,app:prakash}). BIND replicates (IIA \PkGatebOneBind{} at block \PkLstar{} under \fmtNone{}; Gate b1). It exchanges the two binding sentences, changing the state word's ordering information but not its identity; in their account it swaps addresses (read through QK) and a payload (moved through OV).

\paragraph{The binding swap is key-dominant; four predictions failed.}
Without a later mention, keys carry most of BIND's effect and values a substantial share ($\psi_K = \PkPsiKBindNone{}$, $\psi_V = \PkPsiVBindNone{}$, $\kappa = \PkKappaBindNone{}$). Under \textsc{qnames} and \fmtOpt{}, $\kappa = \PkKappaBindQnames{}$ and \PkKappaBindOpt{}, but BIND flips \PkNflipBindQnames{}/\PkN{} answers there, so these decompose a shift of the logit margin; \fmtLetter{} failed the effect-size gate. Not met: values carrying BIND without a later mention ($\psi_V - \psi_K = \HeightDiff$ [$\HeightDiffLo$, $\HeightDiffHi$]; H8); a crossover of at least 0.4 (later mentions raised $\kappa$ by \HnineDiff{} [\HnineDiffLo{}, \HnineDiffHi{}], and \textsc{qnames} is not in between; H9); the rival $\kappa \leq 0.25$ (H10; nor is $\kappa \geq 0.75$ throughout); and the law $\kappa \approx s_{\mathrm{ID}}$, which missed by \HsevenGapMin{}--\HsevenGapMax{} (H7; \cref{fig:frames}c; against $s_{\mathrm{ID}}$ from block 0, secondary: \HsevenGapZeroNone{}, \HsevenGapZeroQnames{}, \HsevenGapZeroOpt{}).

\paragraph{The identity edit, and depth.}
ID at block 0 matched the law ($\kappa_{\mathrm{ID}}$ = \PkKappaIdZeroNone{}, \PkKappaIdZeroQnames{}, \PkKappaIdZeroOpt{} against $s_{\mathrm{ID}}$ = \HelevenSidNone{}, \HelevenSidQnames{}, \HelevenSidOpt{}), and later mentions raised $\kappa_{\mathrm{ID}}$ by \HelevenDiff{} [\HelevenDiffLo{}, \HelevenDiffHi{}] (H11, met). This is consistency on their material, not out-of-sample support: at block 0 ID nearly equals the natural clamp, and H11's block-28 part compares values near zero. Post hoc, at block 28 BIND is key-dominant while ID is value-carried ($\psi_V \geq \PkPsiVIdTwentyeightOpt{}$ in the evaluable formats), a reading consistent with the H7 failure that does not explain it: neither edit flips an answer there when candidates are named, BIND also moves a payload, and Gate b3 failed. Depth is an alternative explanation: under \fmtOpt{}, $s_{\mathrm{ID}}$ falls from \PkSidOptThree{} at block 3 to $\PkSidOptTwentyseven$ at 27 and ID flips no answer from block 20, while BIND flips none below block 24 under \fmtNone{} and at most \PkSweepIIABindQnamesTwentythree{} of answers under \textsc{qnames} or \fmtOpt{}. No depth has both the binding swap and a key-read identity route, so whether the kind of edit or its depth sets how far the format moves the attribution is untested.

\subsection{Preregistered predictions that failed}\label{sec:failed}

A transfer law from clamping-estimated value completeness (C1, C2) failed at Mistral-Small-24B and held only marginally at Qwen2.5-72B; $s_K \geq 0.80$ under \fmtOpt{} (C4) held only at Qwen2.5-32B of the three largest. On the binding swap H7--H10 failed (\cref{sec:prakash}); H6 was not evaluable, and the optional H12 was not run. Of preregistration G, \GcountNotMet{} of 22 failed: G7, G10--G14 and G16 narrow the claims of \cref{sec:readers,sec:heads}, and G19--G21 those on IOI (\cref{sec:general}). \Cref{tab:prereg-g,tab:prereg-h} give every verdict.
