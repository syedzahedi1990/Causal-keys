\section{Whether an edit reaches the answer}\label{sec:claim3}
% Figure fig:window is defined at the head of sections/results_mechanism.tex so that it floats to the top of the page where this section starts.

\textbf{With the candidates listed after the story, an overwrite of the writing token's cache changes the answer only from early layers, up to a model-specific depth; from later layers it leaves the old answer, confidently, although it changes free-form answers until most of the depth.} Single-site edits largely inherit this window.

\paragraph{Design.}
Stage 9 ran Mistral-7B, Llama-3.1-8B and Qwen2.5-7B on \NqStories{} new stories, \NqCores{} new edit cores and the \NqPassages{} passages, scoring generated answers; its predictions were frozen in a dated note before the run (\cref{app:stage9}). The late and early patches (\cref{sec:method}) are applied at every second onset $l$; the \emph{flip} is the share of answers that change to S's, and \emph{listed} answers are those under \fmtListA{} and \fmtLetter. The late window closes at $l_c$, the first onset whose late patch flips fewer than half of the answers. The onset $l_b$ is the first whose late patch flips at most 0.2 of \fmtListA{} answers on \NqCalib{} earlier stories, fixed before the new ones were scored ($l_b/L$ = \NqLbFracMistralSeven, \NqLbFracLlamaEight{} and \NqLbFracQwenSeven). A line holds \emph{at the point estimate} when its estimate, not its 95\,\% interval, clears the threshold. No reading the note pre-wrote for the window applies (\cref{app:s9-note}), so our wording of it is post hoc; ``predicted'' marks the note's lines, judged by the frozen scorer.

\paragraph{The late window.}
Without a later list, the late patch changes at least \NqPoneMinMin{} of the answers from every onset up to half the depth (\fmtNone, \fmtBefore, \fmtNull; predicted; on stories supported, at Llama-3.1-8B at the point estimate; failed on passages, below), and under \fmtNone{} the window closes only at $l_c/L$ = \NqLcNoneMistralSeven/\NqLayersMistralSeven, \NqLcNoneLlamaEight/\NqLayersLlamaEight{} and \NqLcNoneQwenSeven/\NqLayersQwenSeven{} (\cref{fig:window}a--c). With the candidates listed after the story, as words or letters, it closes at $l_c/L$ = \NqLcFracListedMin--\NqLcFracListedMax; under \fmtListA{} that is \NqGapNoneAfterMin--\NqGapNoneAfterMax{} of the depth earlier than under \fmtNone{} (predicted; supported in all three models). The list's position matters more than its presence: with the list before the story the window closes at \NqLcFracBeforeMin--\NqLcFracBeforeMax, later than with it after, though earlier than with no mention. The full patch changes \NqFullMin--\NqFullMax{} of the answers in every format, so listed answers are not sticky.

\paragraph{Late patches fail silently.}
With onset $l_b$, the late patch changes only \NqLateListedMin--\NqLateListedMax{} of listed answers, against \NqLateFreeMin{} without a later list. Of the \fmtListA{} answers it does not change, \pctn{\NqSilentShareAfterMin}--\pct{\NqSilentShareAfterMax} are the old answer B (predicted; supported), with median probability \NqSilentPbAfterMin--\NqSilentPbAfterMax{} (predicted at Mistral-7B only). In \pctn{\NqInconsMin}--\pct{\NqInconsMax} of stories the patch changes the free-form answer and leaves the listed one (reported): an edit validated in free form would pass while deciding nothing in the listed format.

\paragraph{The early window.}
Patching only the layers below $l_b$ changes \NqPthreeEarlyListedMin--\NqPthreeEarlyListedMax{} of listed answers and \NqPthreeEarlyFreeMax{} under \fmtNone{} and \fmtBefore{} (\cref{fig:window}, dashed). The predicted early flip of at least 0.7 with the list was supported at Llama-3.1-8B and Qwen2.5-7B, not at Mistral-7B, whose \fmtListA{} value is the low end of this range. A late cutoff is expected once a token's content has been read elsewhere; the format changes how early, and whether the answer then fails silently. Post hoc, and untested here, this fits \cref{sec:claim2}: with a list the value is read early, at its later mention.

\paragraph{Scope.}
A neutral sentence naming the candidates after the story keeps the window open to half the depth at Qwen2.5-7B, and at Llama-3.1-8B at the point estimate, but at Mistral-7B the late patch from $l_b$ to half the depth changes only \NqPostLateFromLbMinMistralSeven--\NqPostLateFromLbMaxMistralSeven{} of answers, so this prediction (at least 0.8) was not supported. On passages, whether options after the passage close the late window depended on the model, which triggered the note's falsifier; following the note, we report this only as an evaluation warning (whether an intervention changes the answer can depend on the answer format), with no mechanism claim (\cref{app:s9-results}). Two lines failed on passages: the open window with options before at Llama-3.1-8B (\NqPsgLateOptbFortyfiveLlamaEight{} at $0.45L$, against 0.8) and the early window at Qwen2.5-7B (\NqPsgEarlyOptaThirtyQwenSeven, against 0.5).

\paragraph{Single-site edits largely inherit the window.}
A single-site steering vector written at the writing token (E1, the difference of its mean residual between stories with the two values; \citealp{turner2023actadd,panickssery2023caa}) changes as many answers as the natural patch at the same block, within 0.1, in \NqEoneWithinAll{} of \NqEoneCellsAll{} format--block cells (\cref{fig:window}d); the prediction failed through the one other cell ($\NqEoneGapQwenSeven$ at Qwen2.5-7B). A rank-16 subspace remap trained there without a later mention, with the recipe of \citet{anonymous2026fitted} (E4b; Mistral-7B and Qwen2.5-7B), changes \NqEfourbNoneMin--\NqEfourbNoneMax{} of free-form answers, but the ratio $\tau$ of its \fmtListA{} to its free-form flips is \NqTauEfourbThreeMistralSeven{} and \NqTauEfourbThreeQwenSeven{} at block 3, where the natural patch transfers, and \NqTauEfourbBeyondMin--\NqTauEfourbBeyondMax{} beyond (predicted $\tau \leq 0.7$; not supported at Mistral-7B). Steering at every position from the writing token on, the answer and generated tokens included (CAA; \citealp{panickssery2023caa}), differs (reported): at blocks 11 and 15, where the natural patch changes \NqPfivecNatReadMin--\NqPfivecNatReadMax{} of \fmtListA{} answers (Qwen2.5-7B: block 15 only), it changes \NqPfivecCaaMin--\NqPfivecCaaMax{} of them but only \NqEditCaaLetterFifteenQwenSeven--\NqEditCaaLetterFifteenMistralSeven{} of \fmtLetter{} answers, and at block 3 fewer of these than the natural patch (\cref{app:s9-results}). As it also writes at the later mentions and the answer, the window claim is for single-site edits, patching and KV-cache edits only.

\paragraph{Inside the window, the delivered key share predicts transfer.}
An edit's \emph{delivered key share} $r_K$ is the projection of its displacement of the writing token's keys (all heads, layers above the edit) on the natural displacement, divided by the latter's squared norm ($r_K = 1$ for the natural patch). Among edits that change the free-form answer, written where the natural patch still changes at least 0.9 of \fmtListA{} answers (block 3, and 7 at Qwen2.5-7B; steering vectors at four doses, the remap, and cache interpolations, whose $r_K$ is their weight), $r_K$ predicts whether the \fmtListA{} answer changes too (AUC \NqAucMin--\NqAucMax; predicted; supported in all three models). Beyond the window it cannot help: the natural patch itself fails there. The note's half-dose lines failed: at block 3, half-dose edits change \NqHalfAfterMin--\NqHalfAfterMax{} of listed answers, as predicted, but also only \NqHalfNoneMin--\NqHalfNoneMax{} of free-form ones, against at least 0.9; in both formats the response rises steeply past half dose, later with the list (post hoc), and we claim no threshold.

\paragraph{The channel-ratio law failed.}
The final round's law concerned which channel carries an edit, not whether it reaches the answer: one written at depth $l$ would be read through key and value in the model's own ratio there, $\lambda = \log(\psi_K/\psi_V) \approx 0$ ($\psi_C$: its identity effect through channel $C$ relative to the natural write's; \cref{app:law-brief}). Even steering vectors close to the natural edit depart from the natural channel ratio; the format law describes the model's own written value and does not predict how an edit is attributed. % verbatim: C-12 outcome (e), entry J part C
Their departures are small but graded ($\bar\lambda$ = $\JcOnePoolEoneMistralSeven$ to $\JcOnePoolEtwoMistralSeven$ at Qwen2.5-7B and Mistral-7B, inside the $\pm\log 1.25$ margin: equivalence not shown, not departure shown): the format law does not transfer to edits as a channel-ratio law. Edits that change the value through a learned remap are read mainly through the value ($\bar\lambda$ = $\JcFourPoolEfourQwenSeven$ / $\JcFourPoolEfourMistralSeven$): the key channel does not read them as it reads the written word, so the prediction\rsk{} that the remap keeps the model's ratio did not hold; this is relative to the model's own ratio and grows with depth (at block 7 after a sentence $\psi_K$ = \JcFourPsiKWpostSevenQwenSeven{} / \JcFourPsiKWpostSevenMistralSeven{}, $\psi_V$ = \JcFourPsiVWpostSevenQwenSeven{} / \JcFourPsiVWpostSevenMistralSeven{}; post hoc). The law's other lines, most of them risky, did not hold or were not evaluable (\cref{app:law-brief}).
