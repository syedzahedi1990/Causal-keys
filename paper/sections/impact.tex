\section*{Impact Statement}

This work aims to make explanations of language-model internals more reliable, by showing that the component that appears to carry a value or an intervention can depend on the prompt under which it is measured. More reliable component-level explanations could help researchers audit and debug models, and could reduce the risk that a mechanism found under one evaluation format is trusted to explain behaviour under another. Our results also caution against over-reading interpretability findings: an explanation validated under one prompt format should not, on its own, be used to certify a model's behaviour under others. Methods for locating and editing what a model reads from its context could in principle also be used to steer models in unwanted ways, although our experiments use synthetic tasks, and the only learned intervention we study had already been released.
