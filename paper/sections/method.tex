\section{Setup}\label{sec:method}

\paragraph{Stories, runs and formats.}
We use the belief stories of \citet{anonymous2026fitted} (``[\dots] \{agent\} watches as the \{$o$\} is moved to the \{\textsc{loc}\}. [\dots] Where does \{agent\} believe the \{$o$\} is?''; \cref{app:prompts}). Base and source stories B and S differ only at the writing token \{\textsc{loc}\}, at position $p$; a third value X is absent from the story. \fmtOpt{} lists the candidates after the question (``Answer with exactly one choice''), \fmtLetter{} labels them A--F; under ``Answer with one word'', \fmtListA{} lists them there, \fmtPost{} names them in a neutral sentence after the story, \fmtNull{} replaces it by one of the same length that names none, \fmtSentB{} and \fmtBefore{} put the sentence or the list before the story, and \fmtNone{} names none (\emph{free form}). A \emph{later mention} is a token after $p$ that repeats a candidate's word form, up to case. The \emph{fresh stories} are 150 new cores of the same template, with eight new mention sentences, a second location lexicon and per-core candidate orders (\cref{app:fresh}); stage 9 draws \NqStories{} more.

\paragraph{Passages.}
From the SQuAD v1.1 development set \citep{rajpurkar2016squad} we take questions whose answer is a person, place or number named exactly once in the passage, with no content word of it elsewhere, and write a counterfactual entity in its place \citep{longpre2021conflicts}: 185 items from 34 articles, with answers of one to five tokens, clamped at every position of the span. Formats: options after the question (by name or letter) or before the passage, a related-articles sentence after or before it, or free form. We score the first answer token (\cref{app:natural}).

\paragraph{Clamps and patches.}
$\mathcal{C}_K(Z)$ sets the writing token's pre-RoPE key in every layer from $\ell_0$ ($0$ unless stated) to that of run $Z$, and $\mathcal{C}_V(Z)$ its value. Later tokens see the writing token only through these, so clamping both from layer~0 reproduces run $Z$ exactly (checked in FP32; \cref{app:impl}). A single-channel clamp creates a cache state that no prompt produces; generated answers under it show that the read reaches behaviour, not that the state is natural. The \emph{late patch} at onset $l$ clamps both channels to S's from $\ell_0 = l$ to the top layer; the \emph{early patch} clamps both only in the layers below $l$. Together they are the full patch, which reproduces S.

\paragraph{Measures.}
With $\Delta_Y(Z)$ the change in $\log p(Y)$ at the answer under $\mathcal{C}_K(Z)$ against a self-clamp row in the same batch,
\begin{equation}\label{eq:idk}
\mathrm{ID}_K = \tfrac{1}{2}\big[\Delta_{\mathrm{S}}(\mathrm{S}) - \Delta_{\mathrm{S}}(\mathrm{X}) + \Delta_{\mathrm{X}}(\mathrm{X}) - \Delta_{\mathrm{X}}(\mathrm{S})\big],
\end{equation}
that is, how much more a key clamp to S favours S over X than a clamp to X does, averaged symmetrically; effects shared by all foreign keys, and a prior for B, cancel, and it is zero when the key carries no identity. $\mathrm{ID}_V$ is defined likewise with values, $s_{\mathrm{ID}} = \overline{\mathrm{ID}}_K/(\overline{\mathrm{ID}}_K + \overline{\mathrm{ID}}_V)$ is a ratio of means, and $r$ is a format's $\overline{\mathrm{ID}}_K$ relative to that with the list after the story. Fresh stories are scored on the surface forms each model emits (the \emph{emitted-form score}; \cref{app:emitted}). Under each clamp we also decode greedily: $\beta_K$ ($\beta_V$) is the share of answers that move to the clamped value under the key-only (value-only) clamp, net of moves to it under the clamp to the other of S and X; the flip rate omits that correction.

\paragraph{Models and statistics.}
The discovery sample has \nModels{} models: Qwen2.5 at 1.5B--72B \citep{yang2024qwen25}, Qwen3-8B \citep{yang2025qwen3}, Mistral-7B \citep{jiang2023mistral}, Mistral-Small-24B \citep{mistral2025small} and OLMo-2-7B \citep{olmo2025two}; the final round adds Llama-3.1-8B \citep{grattafiori2024llama3}, Gemma-2-9B and Gemma-2-2B \citep{gemma2024gemma2}, Phi-4 \citep{abdin2024phi4} and Falcon3-7B \citep{tii2024falcon3}; stage 9 uses Mistral-7B, Llama-3.1-8B and Qwen2.5-7B. All are instruction-tuned and run in BF16, those of the final round from official weights (sha256-verified; \cref{app:compute}). Intervals are 95\,\% percentile bootstrap intervals, two-stage over clusters on fresh stories, passages and in stage 9, and over stories in the mechanism tests. A prediction over several models must hold in every evaluable model, one over the four new families in three of them (\cref{app:prereg-how}).

\paragraph{Predictions and status marks.}
Each of ten preregistrations was committed before its run, with its scoring code fixed before any output was inspected. Each prediction of the final round had a recorded probability of holding: a \emph{replication} at least 0.9, an \emph{extrapolation} at least 0.8, a \emph{risky} prediction less; where two rules of the entry conflicted, we followed the more specific one (\cref{app:decisions}). In \cref{sec:claim1,sec:claim2}, unless marked, a result was preregistered in the final round and held; \rsk{} marks a risky prediction, failures are said in words, other results are labelled discovery sample, exploratory or post hoc, and \cref{app:crosswalk} maps each sentence to its prediction. \Cref{sec:claim3} reports stage 9, whose predictions were frozen before its run in a dated note, not a preregistration entry, without probabilities; its lines are reported in words, and only its last paragraph, on the final round's channel-ratio law, uses the marks above.
