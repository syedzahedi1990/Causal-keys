\section{Where an intervention appears to act}\label{sec:intervention}

\citet{anonymous2026fitted} read their key exchange between M and P, under \fmtOpt{}, as causal evidence that keys induced by the learned patch contribute to the behavioural difference between the two; we reproduce their result (\cref{app:discussion}). We then change only the format and add a value-only exchange. Two comparisons are confirmatory and met in both models: without a later mention M's keys carry less than half of what they carry under \fmtOpt{} (C3), and M's values carry most of the effect (E4); the rest is exploratory (\cref{tab:frames}).

\paragraph{Behaviour barely changes across formats; the carrying channel flips.}
$\varphi$ lies between \phiOptMistral{} and \phiLetterMistral{} at Mistral-Small-24B and between \phiNoneQwen{} and \phiBeforeQwen{} at Qwen2.5-72B, and the PCA control transfers the source location in every format. Yet at Qwen2.5-72B, M's keys reproduce \psiLetterQwen{} of the M$-$P difference under \fmtLetter{} but \psiNoneQwen{} under \fmtNone{}, while M's values do the reverse (\cref{fig:frames}b). Mistral-Small-24B and the removal fractions (\cref{app:impl}) show the same crossover. Across the ten format--model cells, the remap's key share $\psi_K/(\psi_K+\psi_V)$ tracks the identity key share $s_{\mathrm{ID}}$ of the unpatched model in the same format (\cref{fig:frames}c; $r = \crossR{}$). This is post hoc here, preregistered for the refits below, and failed out of sample for a binding edit (\cref{sec:prakash}).

\paragraph{Interpretation.}
In \fmtLetter{}, \fmtOpt{}, \fmtPost{} and \fmtNone{} the prompt is identical up to the end of the story, so under the causal mask M writes the same key and the same value at the writing token in all four; only their readers differ, and which channel appears to carry the edit follows them. This reading is post hoc (C); its value half (E4) and the refit test below (F) were then preregistered and confirmed. It concerns edits that change which value is written; on the material of \citet{prakash2025lookbacks} we show it only for an identity edit at block 0 (\cref{sec:prakash}).

\paragraph{The readers at 24B.}\label{sec:link}
Preregistration~I tested at Mistral-Small-24B whether the remap is read through the reader heads of \cref{sec:heads} (\cref{tab:prereg-i,app:stage7}). Ranked the same way, the top 5\% of heads recover \IgtwoROpt{} and \IgtwoRLetter{} of the natural key read at the option words under \fmtOpt{} and \fmtLetter{}, and blinding them removes \IgtwoKOOpt{} and \IgtwoKOLetter{} (Gate I-G2). M's key-only effect is read at the option words ($g_K = \IoneGOpt{}$ and \IoneGLetter{}; I1) and through these heads: blinding only them to M's key removes \ItwoKOOpt{} and \ItwoKOLetter{} of that read, random and active sets at most \IthreeMax{} (I2, I3). Mean-ablating them at the option words moves M's key share under \fmtOpt{} from \IfourKappaU{} to \IfourKappaA{}, near its \fmtNone{} level of \IfourKappaNone{} (I4, a consistency check), and M's values carry \IfiveDV{} nats more (I5). Yet M keeps only $t = \IsixT{}$ [\IsixTLo{}, \IsixTHi{}] of its effect on the answer margin (I6, not met against $t \geq 0.75$; the entry's partial takeover), and Gate I-G4, which reading the kept part as a value takeover requires, failed. M's target stays the top answer in \IsixMTA{} of cases (\IsixMT{} unblocked), but under \fmtLetter{} in only \IsixMTALetter{} (\IsixMTLetter{}; reported). Random sets change nothing (I7). Qwen2.5-72B was not run.

\subsection{A remap fit without later mentions}\label{sec:refit}

The remap edits block 4, upstream of every exchanged key and value. Preregistration F asked whether the format it was fit in, rather than its readers, decides its channel: at Mistral-Small-24B it refit the remap with the released code under \fmtNone{} (fit\_none) and, as a same-code control, under \fmtOpt{} (fit\_opt; \cref{app:refit}). All gates and all four predictions were met (\cref{tab:prereg-f,tab:refit}). fit\_none is carried by keys under \fmtLetter{} ($\psi_K = \psiLetterRefNone{}$) and by values under \fmtNone{} ($\psi_V = \psiVNoneRefNone{}$), transfers to lettered options ($\varphi = \phiLetterRefNone{}$), shows the control's crossover (F3), and its key share tracks the natural read ($r = \refRNone{}$; \cref{fig:frames}c). Post hoc, the fitting format still lowers fit\_none's $\psi_K$ under \fmtOpt{} and \fmtPost{} (by \refDiffOpt{} and \refDiffPost{}). The reading format decides which channel dominates.

\subsection{A binding edit from another lab}\label{sec:prakash}

Preregistration H applied the exchange to an edit we did not design: the state-token patch BIND of \citet{prakash2025lookbacks}, at Qwen2.5-14B on their material, with an identity edit ID as a control (\cref{tab:prakash,app:prakash}). BIND replicates (IIA \PkGatebOneBind{} at block \PkLstar{} under \fmtNone{}; Gate b1).

\paragraph{The binding swap is key-dominant; four predictions failed.}
Without a later mention, keys carry most of BIND's effect and values a substantial share ($\psi_K = \PkPsiKBindNone{}$, $\psi_V = \PkPsiVBindNone{}$, $\kappa = \PkKappaBindNone{}$). Under \textsc{qnames} and \fmtOpt{}, $\kappa = \PkKappaBindQnames{}$ and \PkKappaBindOpt{}, but BIND flips \PkNflipBindQnames{}/\PkN{} answers there, so these decompose a shift of the logit margin; \fmtLetter{} failed the effect-size gate. Not met: values carrying BIND without a later mention (H8); a crossover of at least 0.4 (later mentions raised $\kappa$ by \HnineDiff{} [\HnineDiffLo{}, \HnineDiffHi{}], and \textsc{qnames} is not in between; H9); the rival $\kappa \leq 0.25$ (H10; nor is $\kappa \geq 0.75$ throughout); and the law $\kappa \approx s_{\mathrm{ID}}$, which missed by \HsevenGapMin{}--\HsevenGapMax{} (H7; \cref{fig:frames}c).

\paragraph{The identity edit, and depth.}
ID at block 0 matched the law ($\kappa_{\mathrm{ID}}$ = \PkKappaIdZeroNone{}, \PkKappaIdZeroQnames{}, \PkKappaIdZeroOpt{} against $s_{\mathrm{ID}}$ = \HelevenSidNone{}, \HelevenSidQnames{}, \HelevenSidOpt{}), and later mentions raised $\kappa_{\mathrm{ID}}$ by \HelevenDiff{} [\HelevenDiffLo{}, \HelevenDiffHi{}] (H11, met). This is consistency on their material, not out-of-sample support: at block 0 ID nearly equals the natural clamp, and H11's block-28 part compares values near zero. Post hoc, at block 28 BIND is key-dominant while ID is value-carried ($\psi_V \geq \PkPsiVIdTwentyeightOpt{}$ in the evaluable formats), which is consistent with the H7 failure but does not explain it. Depth is an alternative explanation (\cref{app:prakash}): no depth has both the binding swap and a key-read identity route, so whether the kind of edit or its depth sets how far the format moves the attribution is untested.

\subsection{Preregistered predictions that failed}\label{sec:failed}

A transfer law from clamping-estimated value completeness (C1, C2) failed at Mistral-Small-24B and held only marginally at Qwen2.5-72B; $s_K \geq 0.80$ under \fmtOpt{} (C4) held only at Qwen2.5-32B of the three largest. Of the \GHIcountPred{} predictions of G, H and I, \GHIcountFailed{} failed (H6 not evaluable, H12 not run; \cref{tab:prereg-g,tab:prereg-h,tab:prereg-i,tab:failures}): two kept the predicted direction but missed a threshold (G20, H9), six were met in part (G7, G10, G12--G14, G21), one failed on a competence gate (G19), one rejected the rival (H10), and five falsified our prediction (G11, G16, H7, H8, I6). Each of four accounts, ours included, is contradicted somewhere (\cref{tab:accounts}).
