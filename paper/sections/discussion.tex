\section{Discussion}\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{A practical rule, and a hypothesis.}
Which channel carries a written value is set by the tokens after the writing token, so a component-level claim should state where the candidates are mentioned after it, and be tested with and without a later mention; moving them before the writing token is a cheap control that keeps the words but removes the readers (\cref{tab:twobytwo}). A mechanism localised on multiple-choice benchmarks \citep{mueller2025mib} may use another channel than the free-form behaviour it should explain; attributions freezing attention patterns may, we expect (untested), miss the key channel (cf.\ \citealt{kamath2025attention}).

\paragraph{Limitations.}
(i)~Three synthetic, templated tasks with single-token values; on IOI a later list's key read is not established. (ii)~The sentence effect was found post hoc, then preregistered (E1c; its readers D3). (iii)~A single-channel clamp may be off-distribution. (iv)~The fall in key share beyond 14B and (v)~the small key identity under \fmtNone{} and its negative sign under \fmtBefore{} are unexplained. (vi)~The revisit covers two edits: the remap of \citet{anonymous2026fitted} and the binding swap. (vii)~Heads and hop~2 were tested at two 7B models in BF16, in one format; the readers at 24B and 72B are inferred, and (viii)~the same-heads claim across formats is not evaluable (H6). (ix)~The binding swap was tested at one model and flips no answer when candidates are named; (x)~H11's identity edit at block~0 nearly restates the natural clamp; (xi)~edit kind and depth are confounded (Gate~b3).

\paragraph{Conclusion.}
Across four model families and three templated tasks, later mentions of the candidates decide whether a written value's identity is looked up through its key or mainly copied from its value. At two 7B models the lookup has two hops: heads with a duplicate-token core read the key at the re-mentioned words, and the answer reads those words. The released remap of \citet{anonymous2026fitted} and its refit appear to act in the channel the format's readers use; the preregistered extension to another lab's binding edit failed.
