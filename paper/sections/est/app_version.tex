\section*{About this version of the main text}
\addcontentsline{toc}{section}{About this version of the main text}
The main text reports estimates, with intervals, curves and ranges over models; the appendix that follows gives every verdict. The appendix was written to accompany status marks in the main text, which this version does not print: \rsk{} marks a risky prediction (recorded probability of holding below 0.8), and in the verbatim accounts moved to the appendix (\cref{app:claim2-brief,app:law-brief}), whose marks ``are those of the main text'', an unmarked claim was preregistered in the final round and held. Where the appendix refers to the marks (\cref{app:crosswalk}; items 14 and 17 of \cref{app:decisions}) or quotes pre-written wording, the corresponding main-text sentence here states the estimate instead and claims no more. The preamble and item 12 of \cref{app:decisions} place the alternative tally in \cref{sec:discussion}; in this version it is given here. Item 13 describes the law paragraph of the other version; the one here gives the pooled estimates of the steering vectors in all three models, which include those of J-C1 (\cref{app:law-brief}). \Cref{tab:status} gives the tallies (the calibration summary of item 14). If the risky lines were independent, a risky tally as low as the observed \JxRiskyMetABCD{} of \JxRiskyVerdABCD{} or lower would have had probability \JxRiskyTailP{} under the recorded probabilities (post hoc; \cref{app:prereg-j}); under the stricter of two conflicting evaluability rules the risky tally is \JxRiskyMetABCDAlt{} of \JxRiskyVerdABCDAlt{} (expected \JxRiskyExpABCDAlt; \cref{app:decisions}).
